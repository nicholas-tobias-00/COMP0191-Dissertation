# B18 TabICL ensemble extension

## Question

This extension tests whether the B18 ensemble improvement also applies to TabICL, and whether TabICL adds useful error diversity to the final three-forecast TabPFN ensemble. It uses the complete raw daily chains already generated by B18, so no model is approximated and no prior B18 artifact is changed.

Seven complete TabICL forecasts were considered:

- all-history, raw target;
- all-history, tower-robust target;
- all-history with antecedent features, raw target;
- all-history with antecedent features, tower-robust target;
- 1,460-day history, raw target;
- 1,460-day history, tower-robust target;
- tower-robust seasonal experts.

The incomplete original tower-month run was excluded. Its completed pooled-fallback result was already shown to be materially worse and remains preserved separately.

## TabICL-only ensemble result

The best exploratory TabICL-only ensemble is an equal mean of:

1. antecedent features with tower-robust target scaling;
2. the 1,460-day raw-target forecast;
3. the 1,460-day tower-robust forecast.

| Forecast | MASE | MAE | RMSE | R² | Bias |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Best standalone TabICL | 0.7191 | 30.864 | 60.470 | 0.180 | -8.351 |
| Best exploratory TabICL ensemble | **0.7172** | **30.780** | **60.301** | **0.184** | -8.702 |
| Predeclared top-three TabICL mean | 0.7196 | 30.871 | 60.298 | 0.184 | -8.061 |
| Leave-one-block-out subset selection | 0.7302 | 31.405 | 60.924 | 0.167 | -7.573 |
| Forward-only subset selection | 0.7311 | 31.413 | 60.818 | 0.170 | -7.482 |

The exploratory ensemble improves MASE by only 0.0019 over standalone TabICL. A 10,000-resample block bootstrap gives a 95% interval of -0.0081 to 0.0033 for this difference, with the ensemble better in 71.8% of resamples and five of nine individual blocks. The improvement is therefore plausible but not statistically stable.

The leave-one-block-out and forward results are worse because selecting among highly similar TabICL subsets is unstable with only nine evaluable blocks. TabICL ensembling cannot close the material gap to the TabPFN ensemble at MASE 0.6908.

## Mixed TabPFN-TabICL ensemble

The mixed experiment blended the final three-forecast TabPFN ensemble with a TabICL ensemble. TabICL weights from 0% to 50% were evaluated.

The aggregate optimum assigned **0% weight to TabICL**. Leave-one-block-out optimisation also selected 0% TabICL for every held-out block. Thus, under the primary observed-target MASE, TabICL does not contribute complementary predictive information beyond the final TabPFN ensemble.

| Mixed forecast | MASE | MAE | RMSE | R² | Bias |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Final TabPFN ensemble, 0% TabICL | **0.6908** | **29.821** | 60.019 | 0.192 | -10.452 |
| 90% TabPFN + 10% TabICL | 0.6923 | 29.880 | **59.971** | **0.193** | -10.213 |
| 75% TabPFN + 25% TabICL | 0.6952 | 29.989 | 59.930 | 0.194 | -9.854 |
| 50% TabPFN + 50% TabICL | 0.7017 | 30.222 | 59.947 | 0.194 | -9.257 |

A small TabICL contribution marginally improves RMSE, R², and negative bias, but worsens MASE and MAE. This indicates that TabICL slightly moderates some large residuals while degrading typical absolute-error performance.

For the explicit 10% mixture, the MASE difference relative to the TabPFN ensemble is +0.0015. The nine-block bootstrap interval is -0.0006 to 0.0039, and the probability that the mixture is better is only 12.6%. The primary conclusion is therefore to retain the pure TabPFN ensemble.

## Gap-filled-target sensitivity

Across all 2,938 gap-filled evaluation points:

- the selected TabICL-only ensemble has MASE 0.6325;
- the explicit 10% mixed forecast has MASE 0.6057;
- the final pure TabPFN ensemble has MASE 0.6045.

TabICL therefore does not improve the gap-filled-target result either.

## Interpretation

TabICL models are diverse enough that a carefully chosen TabICL-only mean is marginally better than the best individual TabICL forecast. Their errors are not sufficiently complementary to the stronger TabPFN forecasts, however. The MASE-optimal mixed weight is zero, and forcing a non-zero TabICL weight trades slightly better squared-error behaviour for worse absolute scaled error.

The recommended B18 forecast remains the equal three-forecast TabPFN ensemble. The TabICL-only ensemble is useful as a foundation-model sensitivity result, but not as the final operational forecast.
